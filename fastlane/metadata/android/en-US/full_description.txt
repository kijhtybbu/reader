MaiMai Reader (麦麦阅读器) 2.0 — a Material You eBook reader built with Jetpack Compose. Free & Open source & Ad-free, with extensive customization options and support for multiple file formats.

This is a GPL-3.0-only fork of Book's Story by Acclorite (upstream: https://github.com/Acclorite/book-story). The complete source of this fork, including all modifications, is at https://github.com/kijhtybbu/reader. See NOTICE.md for the full upstream and license statement.

Main Features:
* Storage Access Framework
* 7 supported file formats(.pdf, .txt, .epub, .fb2, .html, .htm, .md)
* Material You design with unique themes and color presets
* Organized Library with custom categories and chapters in the Reader
* Highly customizable

Enjoy a sleek, customizable reading app with modern design, built with Jetpack Compose. It’s open-source, offering you a personalized, ad-free journey through your favorite books!
